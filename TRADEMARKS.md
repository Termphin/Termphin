The source code of Termphin is free software under the GNU General Public

and [ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md)). The license covers the code. It does not cover the name **Termphin**, the Termphin logo and icon, or
anything else that identifies the official app (together, "the marks"). Those
belong to Mikołaj Badyl, and this page says how you may use them.

The point is simple: when somebody installs "Termphin", they should get the
app built and published by the Termphin project, not somebody else's build
that only looks like it. For an SSH client - an app that holds keys and
passwords - that matters more than usual.

## You may, without asking

- Fork, study, modify and redistribute the code, as the GPL allows.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on Termphin", "a fork of
  Termphin" or "compatible with Termphin", in plain text, as long as the
  name of your project is not Termphin.
- Write about Termphin - articles, reviews, comparisons, tutorials, videos,
  conference talks - and show the logo and screenshots of the official app
  when you do.
- Link to termphin.dev or to the official store listings with the name and
  logo.
- Build the app from source for yourself, unmodified or not, and use it on
  your own devices.

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish a modified version, or a build you made yourself, under the name
  Termphin or with the Termphin logo or icon - in an app store, on a website
  or anywhere else people download software. A fork that is distributed has
  to have its own name and its own icon, and must not be confusingly similar
  to ours (for example "Termphin Pro", "Termphin+", "Termfin").
- Use the marks in a way that suggests the Termphin project made, reviewed,
  endorsed or supports your product or service.
- Use the marks in a domain name, an app store developer name, a social media
  handle or a company or product name.
- Change the logo or use it as part of your own logo.

## Before you publish a fork

1. Rename the app: the display name, the application id / bundle id
   (`dev.termphin.*`), and the store listing.
2. Replace the launcher icon, the in-app logo and the store graphics.
3. Point links that lead users to us for support (the issue tracker, the
   contact address, the manual) at your own project instead, so your users
   do not ask us about your build.
4. Keep the copyright and license notices - the GPL requires that anyway.

## Asking for permission

Anything not covered here, or anything you are not sure about: write to
**contact@termphin.dev**. Community projects that help Termphin users -
packaging for a Linux distribution, an F-Droid build of the unmodified
source - are usually welcome.

## Reporting misuse

If you find an app or a site using the marks in a way this page does not
allow - especially a lookalike in an app store - please tell us at
**contact@termphin.dev**.
